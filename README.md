## All Addictions

- [💸](#icons) [The Freedom Model](https://www.thefreedommodel.org/freedommodelforaddictions/) - A one-size-fits-all approach, still excellent.
- [💸](#icons) [The SMART Recovery Programme](https://smartrecovery.org.uk/smart-recovery-programme/) - A four step programme that advocates for personal choice.
- [📰](#icons) [Stoic Recovery From Addiction](https://stoicrecovery.com/) - How the stoic philosophy can assist in the recovery process.

## Alcohol

- [💸](#icons) [Kick the Drink Easily](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL17551023W/Kick_the_Drink_Easily) - A book on escaping alcoholism.
- [💸](#icons) [This Naked Mind](https://thisnakedmind.com/) - A programme focusing on a no-shame approach to alcoholism.
- [📰](#icons) ["How Alcoholics Anonymous Steals Your Soul" - Mr. Robert Howard Warner](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Alcoholics-Steals-Your-Soul-Indoctrinating/dp/1489599266) - A potentially good read on how AA often does not work.

## Pornography

- [🆓](#icons) [Burgeon Book](https://www.scribd.com/document/635921955/Burgeon) - A much shorter and condensed e-book on escaping pornography.
- [🆓](#icons) [The Easy Peasy Method](https://easypeasymethod.org/) - An e-book on escaping pornography.
- [🆓](#icons) [The Flying Eagle Method](https://flying-eagle-method.org/) - Another great resource for pornography addiction.
- [📰](#icons) ["My Changes After Quitting Porn Addiction for 7 Months" - C.Smith](https://medium.com/@c_smith/my-changes-after-quitting-porn-addiction-for-7-months-c0ac49d67933) - The author of the Flying Eagle Method wrote about his long-term benefits and changes of escaping porn addictions. Great read!

## Smoking / Vaping

- [💸](#icons) [Allen Carr's Easy Way to Stop Smoking](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Allen-Carrs-Easy-Stop-Smoking/dp/1405923318) - A book on escaping smoking.
